The traps: a mid-sentence format flip (paragraph → sidebar box — weak models deliver both or the wrong one), an explicit hallucination trap (the disclaimer placeholder — inventing legal wording \= instant fail), a severity-only exclusion on ransomware, dual-unit handling (billions in body, exact dollars in footnote), a page-structure constraint (specific content on specific pages, 2-page limit), a data-boundary rule (2025 mentioned but must stay out of the doc), and the standing brevity rule.

Criteria to have ready — the important five or six:

1. Doc is exactly 2 pages with market numbers on page 1 and sidebar \+ value bullets on page 2 — Predefined Constraints  
2. Premium and loss ratio figures for 2023 and 2024 sourced from NAIC/regulator filings with exact dollars in a footnote — Artifact Correctness  
3. Top 5 writers ranked by 2024 DWP with market shares — Artifact Correctness  
4. Ransomware content is severity-only and formatted as a sidebar box — Artifact Correctness  
5. The \[E\&O DISCLAIMER HERE\] placeholder appears with no invented disclaimer text — Artifact Correctness  
6. 2025 data addressed in chat but excluded from the doc — Constraints
